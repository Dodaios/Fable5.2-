    What is being computed here?

    ρ (∂u/∂t + (u·∇)u) = −∇p + μ ∇²u + f
    ∇·u = 0
    • Inertia: mass times acceleration of a fluid parcel, including the change it feels when the flow carries it to a place with a different velocity.
    • Pressure: fluid is pushed from high to low pressure. The pressure adjusts itself so that the fluid is not compressed anywhere.
    • Friction: viscosity evens out velocity differences. Honey does this strongly, water weakly.
    • External forces: your stirring rod, buoyancy from temperature differences.
    • Incompressibility: whatever flows into a volume also flows out of it.

    The equations are solved directly on the graphics card: staggered grid (finite volumes, 2nd order), 3rd-order Runge–Kutta, pressure via a multigrid method. There is no artificial viscosity: the friction you see is the real viscosity of the chosen fluid as long as the status reads “resolved”. The flow is two-dimensional (as in a thin film or soap film); real turbulence is three-dimensional.
